About me

Tomas Restrepo

My name is Tomas Restrepo and I'm a software developer located in Colombia, South America. These days I'm CTO at CData, where I spend my time somewhere between architecture, code review and the occasional late-night debugging session.

I'm the author of Viasfora, a Visual Studio extension with over 200K downloads that adds rainbow braces, keyword colouring and a few other things I wanted for myself. Before CData I was a Senior PFE at Microsoft, and before that an independent consultant. I've been a Microsoft MVP since 1999, across a handful of different technology categories.

This site has been running since long before any of that. It is mostly notes to my future self about whatever I was building at the time — lately that means developer tooling and language models, and for a long stretch before it meant BizTalk, Azure and Kubernetes.
